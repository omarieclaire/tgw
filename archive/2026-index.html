Thanks for another incredible Toronto Games Week!

Stay tuned for next year! Be the first to get TGW news by subscribing to our newsletter:

At Toronto Games Week, you might:

  • Play massive videogames projected on concrete silos at the waterfront!
  • Scour High Park for fungi and butterflies in scavenger hunts!
  • Level up your industry knowledge and contacts!
  • Learn from a game-designer-turned-bestselling-novelist!
  • Play an RPG you found in a Little Free Library!
  • Pop by a picnic for Tamagotchi collectors and friends!
  • Hear a professional 12 piece jazz orchestra play video game soundtrack music!
  • Slay monsters by answering video game trivia, then dance to disco!
  • Marvel at game concept art and hear from the artists who make it!
  • Learn how a game was scaled across a decade of hardware!
  • Play a game with trash in an alt.controller showcase!
  • Dig into the guts of the legendary Torontron indie arcade cabinets!
  • Draw your take on a Pokemon proxy card with a date!
  • Draw something that gets teleported into a playable video game!
  • Collect countries by walking around High Park!
  • Feel like a kid and a beautiful organism under a colourful parachute!
  • Play fifty years of soccer video games at the Royal Ontario Museum!
  • Go on a scavenger hunt themed after a Studio Ghibli deep cut!
  • Learn about open source tools and open social web protocols!
  • Craft a personalized oracle card using imagery and symbols from your subconscious!
  • Create your very own fan merch like ribbon badges, pins, & zines!
  • Stroll and chat with other attendees in a relaxing island environment!
